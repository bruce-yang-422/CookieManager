# Chrome Web Store Description — English

Version: v0.3.0

```text
Cookie Manager lets you organize, search, edit, and export cookies by site, and clear a site's cache and stored data. Handle the current site from the toolbar, or open the dashboard to manage cookies for every site in one place.

SITE CATEGORIES AND MANAGEMENT
• Cookies are grouped by main site, with a clear cookie count for each site.
• Automatic categorization recognizes common services, government domains, and regional portals; you can also create custom categories with your own names and colors.
• Search by site, cookie name, or domain.
• Delete a single cookie, a whole site, or several selected sites at once.

QUICK VIEW AND EDITING
• Switch between "This page" and "Whole site" scope in the toolbar popup.
• Create or edit a cookie's name, value, domain, path, and expiry.
• Adjust Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite attributes.
• Copy a cookie's value, name, name=value, JSON, Netscape, or curl header.

CLEAR CACHE AND SITE DATA
• Clear the current site's cache, for this page only or the whole site, and reload the page automatically.
• From the dashboard, clear specific sites, clear selected sites in bulk, or clear all sites while keeping the ones you choose.
• Choose which data to clear. Only the cache is cleared by default, so you stay signed in.

INSPECT SITE STORAGE
• See how much storage the current site uses in the toolbar popup.
• View, edit, or delete the items a site stores in your browser.

IMPORT AND EXPORT
• Supports JSON, Netscape (cookies.txt), and HTTP Cookie header.
• The import format is detected automatically, with legacy Big5, GB18030, Shift_JIS, and Windows-1252 text encoding support.
• Export to a file or copy to the clipboard for website testing and other tools.
• Export the site list as CSV, JSON, or TXT.
• Import site categories, and back up and restore category settings.

DATA ACCESS AND PRIVACY
• To provide its features, the extension reads and processes cookies locally, which may include login credentials.
• It reads the current tab's URL to show that site's cookies, and organizes the site list by cookie domain. When you clear site data, it also reads the URLs of open tabs to find subdomains to clear along with it.
• When you open the toolbar popup, it locally reads the data the current site stores in your browser to show usage and let you manage it.
• Creating, editing, deleting, clearing, importing, and exporting cookies and site data happen only when you start them.
• Categories, the exclusion list, and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage.
• The developer never receives your cookies, login credentials, site data, or browsing information. This data is never sent to an external server, sold, or shared with third parties.
• Exported data is only downloaded to your device or copied to your clipboard when you ask.
• No account required. No ads, analytics, trackers, or remote code.

INTERFACE
Available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, German, Korean, French, Russian, and Spanish, with dark mode.

For anyone who needs to check sign-in state, test cookie settings, clear a site's cache to fix loading problems, or tidy up browser cookies.

Note: Deleting or editing cookies, or clearing site data, may sign you out, reset preferences, or remove offline data. Exported cookies may contain login credentials, so keep them safe and do not share them.

Website: https://cookie-manager.stack-base.com/
Privacy policy: https://cookie-manager.stack-base.com/privacy.html
```
